The Note Under the Door

You get to your building. Sure enough, there's a folded piece of paper wedged under your door.

It's handwritten, but not in your handwriting. It reads:

"They've been in your apartment before. Check the drawer by your desk. Don't turn on the lights."

You push the door open slowly. In the dark, you feel your way to the desk drawer. Inside, taped to the bottom, is a small USB drive.
